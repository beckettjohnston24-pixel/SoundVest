How the like market works
New likes move songs upward. Likes become less influential after 24 hours and 7 days, allowing values to fall when momentum slows. Each position is valued against the score when you invested. You receive the displayed virtual SV payout only when you sell. Minimum value 25%, maximum 400% of the original amount. Your prior investments start at the market score when this update is installed.
